About this page

Rather than show you a generic portfolio, we built three complete example sites for three different kinds of business — a restaurant, an architecture studio, and a coffee roastery. Each one is a real, working website with its own layout, type, color, and content, not a shared template with the colors swapped.

The point is to give you something concrete to react to. If one of these feels close to right for your business, we can start there. If none of them do, that's useful too — it tells us what to avoid.

How a project actually goes

1

A short conversation about your business

Not a questionnaire — a real conversation about what you do, who it's for, and what the site actually needs to accomplish.

2

One design direction, built in full

We don't send three half-finished mockups to choose between. We build one direction all the way through, using your real content.

3

A round of revisions, together

We sit down and go through it page by page, and adjust what isn't working before anything goes live.

4

Launch, and a plan for upkeep

We hand off a site you can actually update yourself, plus a straightforward arrangement if you'd rather we handle changes.

Tell us about your business and we'll tell you what we'd build.
