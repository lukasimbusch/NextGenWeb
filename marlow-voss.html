How we work

How we work with a client

We start with how you actually live or operate

Before any drawings, we spend two to three weeks understanding daily use — not just the wish list, but the habits underneath it.

One point of contact throughout

A single project architect stays with you from concept through construction administration. No hand-offs between phases.

We build what we draw

We stay engaged through construction and visit the site weekly, so decisions made on paper hold up in the field.

Start a project

We take on eight to ten new projects a year. Tell us about the site and the problem you're trying to solve, and we'll set up a first conversation.
