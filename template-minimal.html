About

I started photographing my grandmother's village on weekends and never really stopped. Ten years later, that same patience shapes every project — I spend time before I lift the camera, and I only publish what still feels true a month later.

Work has appeared in regional print titles and has been shown in two small gallery exhibitions in Lisbon.
