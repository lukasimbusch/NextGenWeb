Coffee, sourced on purpose

We started Northbound out of a garage with a 1kg sample roaster and a spreadsheet of farms we wanted to work with directly. Ten years later the roaster is bigger, but the spreadsheet approach hasn't changed — we still buy from the same six farms, and we still visit each one at least once a year.

Every bag tells you the farm, the elevation, and the processing method. If a lot doesn't meet the bar we set on the cupping table, it doesn't go out under our name — it's sold off green instead.
